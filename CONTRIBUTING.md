# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve **Awesome AI Agents**.

## Adding a resource

1. Open a pull request that adds **one resource per line** to the most relevant section.
2. Use this format: `- [Name](https://link) — one-line description of what it is and why it's useful.`
3. Keep descriptions short, neutral and factual. No marketing language.
4. Check the link works and isn't already in the list.

## What gets accepted

- Actively maintained tools, frameworks, papers and guides about AI agents.
- Things you have actually used or read, not just ones that look interesting.

## What doesn't

- Dead or paywalled links, affiliate links, and self-promotion with no substance.

## Fixing things

Broken links, typos and miscategorized entries are always welcome. Just open a PR or an issue.
